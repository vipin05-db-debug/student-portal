Keep Your Money Safe

Beware of anyone requesting fee payments through unofficial channels like their personal account or QR code etc. as they may be fraudsters. To safeguard your money, please keep these important points in mind:

- Only pay your re-registration fee through the official channels like student portal.
- The university does not provide any discounts on re-registration fees.
- Reach out to the university helpdesk team if you are in any doubt.
